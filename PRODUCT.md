# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Independent SQL learners, beginners through advanced analysts, and classrooms. Readers use phones and desktops, keyboards and assistive technology; neither registration nor an instructor should be necessary for solo play.

## Product Purpose

A connected detective investigation that teaches real PostgreSQL through reproducible queries, actionable diagnostics and explicitly filed evidence. MVP means 24 playable missions, not the full 80-mission season. CP0a is only the static foundation.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, humane. Port Meridian's Municipal Records Investigation Unit values evidence over guesses. Mood and story frame the learning without obscuring the task.

## Anti-references

Avoid suspect-name guessing, punitive timers, hint economies, mandatory accounts, decorative dashboards, fake playable controls and claims of offline/save readiness before those behaviors exist. No competitor art or copied narratives.

## Design Principles

- Explain what a query proves, not just whether it passed.
- Preserve agency and saved work; do not hide failures.
- Make the next action legible without overwhelming the learner.
- Keep runtime local, same-origin and account-free.
- Represent unfinished capabilities honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target, keyboard navigation, 320–360px reflow, reduced motion, clear text states and manual NVDA/VoiceOver/TalkBack/device audits. English MVP with stable locale keys later; SQL identifiers stay in English. The normative source is [spec §§1,6,13–15](docs/spec/SPEC-001-The-Quiet-Index-v1.1.md); this context does not introduce a new product direction.
